# Project 2 Mid-Point Review:

## 3D Printer Customization

We reconfigured a Prusa i3 MK3S 3D printer by disconecting the printer's x, y, and z-axes and cutting the connectors off. We then crimped the wires and attached new connectors so that the printer is compatible with the Stepdance board.
