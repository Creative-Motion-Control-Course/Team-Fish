We aim to continue working to provide support for computer and plotter aided embossing, further developing our current framework. The envisioned practitioners of our tool include hobbyists, crafters, printmakers, and creative practitioners of all kinds. Our CNC embossing tool seeks to extend and enhance hand tool embossing using styluses and ball tools on foil. With our CNC, we plan to extend the skills of pattern design and layout with digital precision and repeatability, depth and pressure control, as well as possibly scaling and mirroring designs.
